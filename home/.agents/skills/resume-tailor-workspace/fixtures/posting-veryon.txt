Director of Engineering (AI Adoption) | Veryon | Fully Remote — Canada or USA
Job Type: Full-time

ABOUT VERYON
Veryon is the leading global provider of aviation software and information services, trusted by over 5000+ customers, 75,000 maintenance professionals, and 100+ OEMs across nearly 150 countries. Our mission is to deliver smarter, predictive technology solutions, including our AI platform, Veryon AIRE, that maximizes aircraft uptime and operational efficiency for the world's most demanding aviation organizations.

At Veryon, we are an AI-forward company focused on driving innovation and efficiency through emerging technologies. We prioritize hiring individuals who embrace AI, think creatively about its application, and are excited to continuously evolve alongside it.

ABOUT THE ROLE
Veryon is seeking a Director of Engineering (AI Adoption) to lead the strategy, development, and adoption of artificial intelligence initiatives both externally in our products and internally across the organization.

Externally this role will drive the evolution of Veryon's product portfolio specifically focusing on initiatives involving Veryon AIRE. This includes the full lifecycle through analysis, design, software development, sales & support enablement, market launches, and most importantly supporting top tier service to large enterprise customers as they roll out Veryon AIRE solutions across their maintenance operations.

Internally this role will be responsible for running AI initiatives across all departments ensuring that we are embedding AI into the daily lives and processes of our teams in a way that drives business value. This role must partner closely with the Executive Leadership Team (ELT), Senior Leadership Team (SLT) and Security and Compliance leaders to define and execute function-specific AI roadmaps that adhere to the strict AI usage policies at Veryon.

This role requires a combination of strategic vision, technical depth, and the ability to lead cross-functional transformation within a complex aviation environment.
